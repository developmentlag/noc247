Monitoring Scope

What our NOC covers

Six core monitoring pillars, each backed by your runbooks and triage priorities. Turn on what you need, add client-specific monitors, scale as your MSP grows.

Servers

CPU, RAM, disk, services, event logs, uptime, and service health — triaged per your runbooks.

Network Devices

Firewalls, switches, routers, WAN/MPLS links, uptime, latency, and interface flapping.

Firewalls

VPN tunnel status, rule change audits, throughput anomalies, and firmware update windows.

Backups

Job success/failure verification, backup copy health, restore-testing cadence tickets.

Patching

OS & third-party patching in approved overnight windows, post-patch health checks, rollback.

AV / EDR

Threat alert triage, AV definition out-of-date remediation, quarantine follow-up tickets.
