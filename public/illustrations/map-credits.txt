Case-study night maps

Coastlines and country borders are Natural Earth 1:50m
(ne_50m_land and ne_50m_admin_0_countries).
Source: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/
License: public domain. https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

City positions are the geographic coordinates of Dallas, Houston, Austin,
London, Manchester, Edinburgh, Sydney, Perth, Amsterdam, Brussels and
Frankfurt, plus a few dimmer nearby cities for context.

The navy tint, graticule and city-light glow are an original rendering
for NOC247. No NASA imagery is included.

Regenerate with: node scripts/render-region-maps.mjs
(expects the two GeoJSON files in /tmp/ne, or NE_DIR).
