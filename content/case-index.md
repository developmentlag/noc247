# MSP NOC case studies: scenarios by region

Most MSPs reading a NOC case study want one answer: has someone with my stack, my size and my hours problem done this, and what did the first month look like?

We can't show you a client result here yet. No partner has signed off figures for publication, so each page below is an illustrative scenario. It starts with a realistic regional MSP profile, then walks through our onboarding, runbook, escalation and morning-report process as it would run for that MSP. Everything in the profile is an assumption, and nothing on these pages is a client result.

When a partner approves published figures, they will go on the matching page with the period and the method, and the scenario label will stay on the pages that remain scenarios.

## What every scenario covers

Each page follows the same order, so you can compare them:

- **The scenario at a glance.** The MSP's size, stack and hours today, in a table of model inputs.
- **The gap.** What happens when the day desk goes home.
- **Onboarding.** Discovery, monitor audit, runbook and priority table, shadow shifts, go-live and 30-day calibration.
- **One modelled night.** An hour-by-hour table showing which tier acts and when a person is called.
- **Permissions and escalation.** What the NOC may do under the MSP's runbook, and what always stays with the MSP.
- **What is specific to the region.** Time zones, holidays, regulation and on-call habits.
- **The morning report and how success would be measured.** Metric definitions first, so numbers mean something when they exist.

## How to read the numbers

Endpoint counts, team sizes and times in these pages are model inputs. They show the shape of the work, not an outcome. You won't find a percentage improvement or a "hours saved" figure on any of them, because we have none to report.

The better use of these pages is to compare the scenario to your own estate. If your stack and your gap look similar, the [free After-Hours Alert Audit](/free-alert-audit) will show how much of your own overnight alert volume needs a person.

## FAQ (for FAQPage schema)

**Are these real client case studies?**
No. They are illustrative scenarios. Each one is built from a realistic regional MSP profile and NOC247's published onboarding, runbook, escalation and reporting process. The figures are model inputs, not results.

**Why publish scenarios instead of client results?**
Because no partner has approved published figures yet. We would rather show the method clearly than invent a number, and an MSP evaluating a NOC can still see how the process works.

**Will you add real results later?**
Yes, when a partner approves them. Real results will state the period, how each figure was measured and which tools were involved.

**Which tools do the scenarios use?**
Each scenario assumes a stack the MSP already runs, such as ConnectWise, NinjaOne, Datto RMM, N-able N-central, Autotask, HaloPSA and Auvik, with a backup product such as Veeam, Acronis, Datto BCDR or N-able Cove. The RMM and PSA tools NOC247 works inside are listed on [RMM integrations](/rmm-integrations). Your backup product is confirmed during scoping.
